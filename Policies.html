School Policies

Guidelines and policies for students, parents, and staff

Attendance Policy

Students are expected to attend all scheduled classes on time. Absences must be reported to the school office and supported by a valid excuse letter or medical certificate. Habitual tardiness or unexcused absences may result in a conference with the guidance office.

Dress Code

Students are required to wear the prescribed school uniform on regular school days. Proper grooming and appropriate footwear are expected at all times. Physical education attire should only be worn on scheduled PE days.

Academic Integrity and Grading Policy

Students are expected to complete all academic requirements honestly and independently. Cheating, plagiarism, and other forms of academic dishonesty are strictly prohibited and subject to disciplinary action. Grades are computed based on written work, performance tasks, and quarterly examinations.

Enrollment Requirements

Enrollment requires submission of a duly accomplished enrollment form, report card or Form 138, birth certificate, and other documents as required by the school registrar. Continuing students must settle any outstanding requirements from the previous school year prior to enrollment.

Code of Conduct

Students are expected to conduct themselves with respect, honesty, and responsibility both inside and outside the classroom. Bullying, vandalism, and other forms of misconduct are not tolerated and will be addressed according to the school's disciplinary procedures.
